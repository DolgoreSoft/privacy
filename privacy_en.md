# Dahari Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 August 2026  
**Operator:** Dolgoresoft  
**App:** Dahari (`com.dolgoresoft.dahari`)  
**Contact:** main@dolgoresoft.com  
**Korean:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)

Dahari helps non-native Korean speakers practice job interviews in Korean. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it, and who we share it with.

You may add a **business address** to this document if you have one.

---

## 1. Information we collect

| Category | Data | When |
|----------|------|------|
| Account | Identifiers, email, name, and profile photo provided by Google or Apple Sign-In (if available) | Sign-in |
| Profile | Display name, target job role | Onboarding and profile edits |
| Interview history | Difficulty, job role, question text, answer text (from speech), AI feedback and scores | After a completed interview |
| Audio | Microphone recordings. If on-device speech recognition works, we keep **text only**. If it does not, we send the recording to Google Gemini to create text. | While you record an answer |
| Device TTS | The “listen” button for improved answers uses the phone’s speech engine. | Feedback listen |
| Ads | Advertising identifiers and device/app data collected by Google’s ads SDK | Interstitial before an interview |
| Demo mode | Demo data stays on this device only. | Demo use |

We do not collect location, contacts, photo library, or calendar data.

## 2. How we use it

- Sign-in and account management
- Interview questions matched to your job role
- Turning spoken answers into text and giving grammar/content feedback
- Playing interviewer audio (question **text** is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech)
- Saving history for signed-in users across devices
- Showing ads so the app can stay free
- Fixing problems and improving the service

## 3. Retention and deletion

- **Profile and interview history:** Until you delete your account or ask us to delete it, unless a law requires a longer period.
- **Audio files:** Deleted on the device after transcription. Audio sent to Gemini is handled under Google’s policies.
- **Demo data:** Deleted on the device when you leave demo mode. It is not uploaded.
- **Ads data:** Governed by Google’s ads policies.

## 4. Sharing, processors, and international transfers

We use these providers. Processing may occur in the **United States and other regions where Google operates**.

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|----------|---------|------|
| Google Firebase Authentication | Sign-in | Account identifiers |
| Google Cloud Firestore | Profile and history storage | Profile, questions, transcripts, feedback |
| Google Gemini (Generative Language API) | Transcription when needed; learning feedback | Question and answer text; audio if needed |
| Google Cloud Text-to-Speech | Interviewer voice | Question text |
| Google AdMob | Interstitial ads | Ad and device identifiers |
| Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple | Sign-in | Account data those companies provide |

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described here or as required by law.

Provider policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

## 5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.

- You can edit your profile in the app.
- You can sign out or leave demo mode to clear that session’s local data.
- For other requests, email us at the contact above.
- You can unlink Google or Apple in those account settings.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or a similar region, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 6. Children

Dahari is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 where that is the applicable age). We delete information if we learn it belongs to a child below that age.

## 7. Advertising

We show Google AdMob interstitials to keep the app free. On iOS, declining tracking permission may limit personalized ads. You can also turn off ad personalization in device settings.

## 8. Security

- HTTPS in transit
- Firestore access limited to the signed-in user’s own documents
- Audio files removed from the device after transcription

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## 9. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Microphone | Record interview and phrase-practice answers |
| Speech recognition | Convert Korean speech to text on the device |
| Internet | Sign-in, AI feedback, question audio, ads, history sync |

Denying a permission only limits that feature. Written feedback and other parts of the app can still work.

## 10. Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Material changes may also be noted in the app or store listing.
